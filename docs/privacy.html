Privacy Policy

RemVol Booster · Version 1

Short version: RemVol Booster does not collect, store, or share your personal data.
What you do in the app stays on your device.

1 · About this app

RemVol Booster is a free, lightweight volume booster for Android. It works entirely on your device. It does not require an account, does not ask you to sign in anywhere, and does not contain tracking of its own. There is no registration, no profile, and no user database.

2 · Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app does not have server-side storage and does not transmit your usage data to us. Your settings (for example the boost limit, smart boost toggle, or start-on-boot preference) are saved locally on your device only, and never leave it.

3 · Permissions

The app only asks for the permissions that are strictly needed to do its job:

  • Audio settings — to adjust and boost the device volume.
  • Run at startup — only if you enable "Start on boot" in Settings.
  • Notifications — to show a small status notification while boosting (optional).
  • Network access — used solely to load advertisements and to check this GitHub page for app updates.

RemVol Booster does not access your contacts, photos, files, location, microphone, or camera.

4 · Advertisements (Google AdMob)

The app displays ads served by Google AdMob. Google may use device identifiers and basic technical data to deliver and measure ads. This is governed by Google's own policies, which you can read here: Google Privacy & Terms. You can control ad personalization through the "Ad preferences" option inside the app or through your Android system settings.

5 · Update checks

To let you know when a new version is available, the app can contact the public GitHub releases page of this project. This request contains no personal data — only a check for the newest published release.

6 · Children

RemVol Booster is a general-purpose utility and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

7 · Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this same page. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
